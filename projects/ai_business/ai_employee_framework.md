Status:
Core Product Strategy

Version:
1.0

---

## Vision

Businesses should not buy AI.

Businesses should hire AI Employees.

Every AI Employee has one clear job and delivers measurable business value.

---

## Core Principles

1. Sell employees, not AI.

2. Every AI Employee has:
   - Role
   - Rules
   - Workflow
   - Skills
   - Knowledge Base
   - Memory (where appropriate)

3. Every AI Employee must be tested before deployment.

4. Every AI Employee should work across multiple channels.

Examples:

- WhatsApp
- Website
- Facebook
- Instagram
- Telegram
- Voice (future)

5. Measure business outcomes instead of technical features.

Businesses care about:

- More leads
- More bookings
- More sales
- Faster replies
- Better customer support
- Lower operating costs
- 24/7 availability
