# Law 001 (Draft)

## Definition of a Relationship

Status:
Draft

---

### Question

What is a relationship?

---

### Proposed Definition

A relationship exists whenever a change in one entity can influence,
constrain, describe, or predict a change in another entity.

Without interaction or dependency,
there is no relationship.

---

### Examples

Mass ↔ Gravity

Voltage ↔ Current

Supply ↔ Price

Neuron ↔ Neuron

Planet ↔ Star

Human ↔ Society

---

### Properties

A relationship may be

- Constant
- Variable
- Direct
- Inverse
- Symmetric
- Asymmetric
- Local
- Global

---

### Prediction

Every measurable phenomenon can be represented as one or more relationships.
