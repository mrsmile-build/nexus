# NEXUS Task Planner

Every goal is divided into smaller tasks.

Example

Goal:
Build a bridge.

↓

Research
↓

Design

↓

Simulation

↓

Verification

↓

Construction

↓

Testing

↓

Finished

Planner Rules

- Break large goals into small goals.
- Estimate difficulty.
- Identify dependencies.
- Choose tools.
- Verify completion.
