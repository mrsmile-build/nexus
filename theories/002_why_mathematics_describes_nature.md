Status:

## Question

Why is mathematics so effective at describing reality?

Is mathematics invented by humans?

Or is mathematics the language of relationships that already exist in nature?

---

## NEXUS Hypothesis

Everything that exists can be represented as relationships.

Objects are not fundamental.

Relationships are fundamental.

Mathematics is simply the language that measures relationships.

If two systems have identical relationships,
they should obey identical mathematics,
even if they appear completely different.
