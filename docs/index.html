**Reasoning** What we know: Blended cement replaces part of the high‑energy clinker with supplementary cementitious materials (SCMs) such as fly ash, slag, or limestone. This lowers material cost and CO₂ emissions while still attaining compressive strengths comparable to ordinary Portland cement (OPC) when the blend is properly proportioned. Dangote’s low‑cost cement uses a high SCM share, showing commercial viability. What is uncertain: The optimal SCM type and replacement level for a specific local market (raw‑material cost, availability, and required strength class). Also, the impact of SCM fineness and curing regime on early‑age strength versus long‑term performance. **Concrete next action** – Conduct a short literature review (e.g., ASTM C595, EN 197‑1) to identify proven clinker‑SCM ratios that achieve target 28‑day compressive strength at ≤ 70 % of OPC cost, then plan a pilot‑batch trial using locally sourced fly ash and limestone.
